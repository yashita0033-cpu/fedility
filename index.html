About Fidelity Dawakhana

Fidelity Dawakhana started in 2008 from a small shop in Green Park Market, Lucknow. What began as a regular medical store slowly grew into a place where families from the neighbourhood come not just for medicines but also for reliable advice.

We are a family-run business. The store is currently managed by Mr. Rakesh Mehra (Registered Pharmacist) along with two other qualified staff members. Over the years we have built long-term relationships with authorised distributors so that we can keep a steady supply of both common and less commonly demanded medicines.

What we believe

Medicines are not ordinary products. Wrong medicine, wrong dose or expired stock can cause serious harm. That is why we follow a simple rule: if we are not sure about a product’s authenticity or condition, we do not put it on the shelf.

We also understand that many customers, especially elderly patients and those on long-term medication, prefer dealing with the same familiar faces. Our staff has been with us for several years and knows many of the regular customers by name.

Our range

  • Allopathic medicines – prescription and OTC
  • Ayurvedic classical and proprietary products
  • Homeopathic dilutions, mother tinctures and combinations
  • Unani medicines and formulations
  • Surgical disposables, first-aid and diagnostic devices
  • Baby care, nutritional supplements and personal care items

Licences & compliance

We hold a valid retail drug licence issued by the Uttar Pradesh State Drug Control Administration. We maintain proper records for Schedule H and Schedule X drugs as required under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act. GST registration is also in place.

Prescription medicines are dispensed only against a valid prescription from a registered medical practitioner. We do not encourage self-medication for serious conditions.

Looking ahead

In the coming months we plan to improve our home delivery coverage and introduce a simple WhatsApp-based ordering system for regular customers. The core of the business, however, remains the same — a physical store where people can walk in, ask questions and leave with the right medicine.

If you have any feedback or suggestions, please write to us at care@fidelitydawakhana.in or visit the store. We take customer feedback seriously.
